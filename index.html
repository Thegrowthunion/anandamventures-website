FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before working with us.

What exactly does the 90-day model cover?
The 90-day model is a complete business transformation programme. It covers operations audit, menu engineering, food cost optimisation, staff training, digital marketing, revenue systems, and handover. We work on-site with your team — not just advise from a distance.
How much does the 90-day programme cost?
Pricing depends on the size and type of your business. The initial consultation is always free. After we understand your needs, we'll give you a transparent, customised quote. The investment is always significantly less than the revenue increase you'll see.
Do you work outside of Haryana?
Yes. While our primary focus is North India — Haryana, Punjab, Delhi NCR, and UP — we work with clients across India. Some services like digital strategy and consulting calls can be done remotely for clients in other cities.
What is the Lahori Adda franchise investment?
The Lahori Adda franchise investment starts at ₹10 Lakhs, all-inclusive. This covers the franchise fee, setup, initial stock, and working capital buffer. The projected payback period is 18–30 months depending on location and footfall.
Can you help us if our restaurant is already struggling?
Absolutely. Turnarounds are one of our specialties. We've taken restaurants from near-closure to profitability. The 90-day model was specifically designed for struggling businesses — and the results speak for themselves.
Do you help with staff hiring and training?
Yes — this is a core service. We recruit hospitality staff (chefs, managers, service staff, cashiers) and train them according to your brand standards. We also document SOPs so your team performs consistently even after we leave.
How long before we see results?
Most clients see measurable improvement in revenue and operations within the first 30–45 days. The full transformation is visible by Day 60–90. We commit to a minimum 25% revenue improvement by the end of the programme.
Do you also build websites for restaurants?
Yes. We design and build custom websites for restaurants, hotels, cafes, and hospitality businesses — optimised for Google search, online bookings, and conversions. Pricing starts at competitive rates and the website is handed over to you fully.
