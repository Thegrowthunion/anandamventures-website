FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before working with us.

How does your consulting engagement work?
Every engagement starts with a free consultation and a full, honest business audit. We then build a customised strategy specific to your business and work on-site with your team to implement it — covering operations, menu, staff, digital presence, and revenue systems.
How much does the programme cost?
Pricing depends on the size and type of your business. The initial consultation is always free. After understanding your needs, we give a transparent customised quote. Our fee is always less than the revenue increase you'll see.
Do you work outside Haryana?
Yes. Our primary focus is North India — Haryana, Punjab, Delhi NCR, and UP — but we work with clients across India. Some services like digital strategy can be done remotely for clients in other cities.
What is the Lahori Adda franchise investment?
Starting at Contact us for details all-inclusive — franchise fee, setup, initial stock, and working capital. Projected payback is 18–30 months depending on location and footfall.
Can you help if our restaurant is struggling?
Absolutely. Turnarounds are one of our specialties. We've taken restaurants from near-closure to profitability. Our structured consulting approach was designed for struggling businesses — and the results speak for themselves.
How soon will we see results?
Most clients see measurable improvement in weeks 5–8. Full transformation is visible by end of engagement. Our engagements consistently deliver meaningful revenue improvement by end of the programme.
Do you help with staff hiring?
Yes — this is a core service. We recruit hospitality staff (chefs, managers, service staff, cashiers) and train them to your brand standards. We also document SOPs so your team performs consistently after we leave.
Do you build websites for restaurants?
Yes. We design and build custom websites for restaurants, hotels, cafes, and hospitality businesses — optimised for Google search, online bookings, and conversions. Competitive pricing, handed over fully to you.
