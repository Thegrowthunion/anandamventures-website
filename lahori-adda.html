The Recipe's Origin

Our Story

A recipe that
survived partition.


When India was divided in 1947, millions of families crossed the border leaving everything behind — their homes, their cities, their streets. But they carried something that could not be taken away: their recipes.

The chole bhature you find on the streets of Delhi and Haryana today is a direct descendant of Lahori cuisine — brought across by families who had been making it for generations. The deep, slow-cooked chole, the perfectly puffed bhatura fried in the right oil at the right temperature — this is not street food. This is culinary heritage.

"Uss paar ki yaad, iss paar ka swad."

The memory of that side, the taste of this side.

Lahori Adda is built on this story. Backed by 35 years of hospitality expertise from Anandam Ventures, we are bringing this heritage recipe to a franchise format — consistent, authentic, and built to scale across Haryana.

FAQs

Franchise Questions

Everything you need to know before investing.

What is the total franchise investment?
The all-inclusive franchise investment ranges from ₹10 to ₹20 Lakhs depending on outlet size and location. This covers the franchise fee, full setup, initial inventory, working capital buffer, brand kit, training, and digital setup. No hidden costs.
How long does it take to open after signing?
Typically 30–45 days from agreement signing to opening day — depending on your location's readiness and equipment procurement. Our team works alongside you throughout to make this as fast as possible.
Do I need prior hospitality experience?
No. We have trained complete first-timers to run successful outlets. Our 90-day onboarding covers everything — from kitchen operations to customer service to digital marketing. Motivation and capital matter more than prior experience.
What is the projected payback period?
18 to 30 months, based on conservative projections. Franchisees in high-footfall locations have recovered their investment faster. These are projections — actual results depend on location, management, and local market conditions.
How does the masala supply work?
Our proprietary masala blend is prepared at our central kitchen in Panipat and supplied to all franchisees on a regular schedule. You cannot replicate the recipe independently — this protects the brand's consistency and your competitive advantage.
What ongoing support do I receive after opening?
Full 90-day on-ground support after opening. After that, ongoing support via WhatsApp franchise community, monthly performance review calls, and access to our team for any issues. You are never managing alone.
Is there a minimum monthly purchase commitment?
Yes — there is a minimum monthly supply purchase commitment as per the franchise agreement. This ensures brand standards are maintained and supply chain integrity is preserved. The minimum is set realistically based on your outlet size.
Can I open multiple outlets?
Yes. Multi-outlet franchisees get preferential terms on subsequent outlets. We encourage successful franchisees to expand within their territory or into adjacent cities. Several of our existing partners are already planning their second outlets.
