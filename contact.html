What is Anandam Ventures? About Us
Anandam Ventures is a multi-venture hospitality company based in Panipat, Haryana. Founded by Mr. Yudhishter Anand with 35 years of hospitality industry experience, we offer three things: hospitality consulting services for restaurants, hotels and food businesses; Lahori Adda — our own authentic chole bhature franchise brand; and custom website design for hospitality businesses.
Who are the founders of Anandam Ventures? About Us
Anandam Ventures is co-founded by Mr. Yudhishter Anand and Mrs. Parveen Anand. Mr. Anand brings 35+ years of hospitality experience, having worked with iconic brands including Sukhdev Dhaba (Murthal), Haveli (Karnal), and currently serving as General Manager at Ahuja Sweets, Panipat. Mrs. Parveen Anand leads people, culture and internal operations. Together they bring both operational expertise and values-driven leadership.
What type of businesses do you work with? About Us
We work with hotels, restaurants, cafes, QSRs (quick service restaurants), dhabas, sweet shops, bakeries, and cloud kitchens — across all stages: new launches, growing businesses, and struggling operations needing a turnaround.
Where are you based and do you work outside Haryana? About Us
Our office is in Panipat, Haryana. Our primary market is North India — Haryana, Punjab, Delhi NCR, and Uttar Pradesh. We also work with clients across India, and for services like digital marketing and website design, we work fully remotely for clients in any city.
What consulting services do you offer? Services
We offer six core services: (1) Restaurant Launch & Setup — concept to opening day; (2) Revenue Growth & Turnaround — fixing struggling businesses; (3) Staff Hiring & Training — building high-performance hospitality teams; (4) Marketing & Digital Growth — Google, Zomato, Swiggy, social media; (5) Expansion & Scaling — franchise models and multi-outlet growth; (6) Website & Digital Presence — custom restaurant websites. See our Services page for full details.
Is the first consultation really free? Services
Yes, completely. We offer a free 30-minute consultation — no charge and no commitment. We understand your business, your challenges, and your goals. After that, if you want to proceed, we give you a transparent quote. There is no obligation to hire us after the consultation.
Do you work with first-time restaurant owners? Services
Absolutely — many of our best outcomes have been with first-time owners. You don't bring bad habits; we bring 35 years of expertise. We handle everything from concept to grand opening so you open with confidence, not guesswork.
Can I hire you for just one specific service? Services
Yes. All six services are available as standalone projects — you can hire us just for a website, just for staff training, just for marketing setup, or just for a new outlet launch. You don't need to take the full 90-day programme if you only need targeted help.
What is the 90-Day Transformation Model? 90-Day Model
Our 90-Day Transformation Model is a structured, three-phase programme: Phase 1 (Days 1–30) — complete audit and strategy blueprint; Phase 2 (Days 31–60) — implementation of all systems, retraining, menu revamp, digital overhaul; Phase 3 (Days 61–90) — scaling, corporate tie-ups, performance review and 6-month growth roadmap handover. We work on-site with your team throughout. See the full model on our homepage.
What results does the 90-day model guarantee? 90-Day Model
We commit to a minimum 25% revenue improvement by Day 90. If we don't deliver, we continue working without additional charge until we do. This commitment is included in the engagement agreement. Our average result across clients is 25–40% revenue growth and 15–20% food cost reduction.
How much does the 90-day programme cost? 90-Day Model
Pricing is customised based on your outlet size, type, and location. The consultation is always free. We provide a transparent, fixed-cost quote before any commitment. As a general note — our fee is always significantly less than the incremental revenue you will gain during the programme itself.
How soon will I see results after starting? 90-Day Model
Most clients see measurable improvement — higher daily sales, lower food costs, better online visibility — within weeks 5 to 8. The full, sustained transformation is visible by Day 60–90. Some quick wins (like Zomato listing improvements and staff training) often show results within the first two weeks.
What is Lahori Adda? Lahori Adda
Lahori Adda is our own authentic chole bhature franchise brand — rooted in a 1947 Lahori recipe that crossed the border during partition. We are currently franchising 25 outlets across Haryana's NH-44 corridor in Phase 1. Each outlet is backed by our central kitchen masala supply, brand support, and 90-day onboarding. Learn more on the Lahori Adda page →
What is the total franchise investment? Lahori Adda
The all-inclusive Lahori Adda franchise investment ranges from ₹10 to ₹20 Lakhs depending on outlet size and location. This includes franchise fee, full setup, brand kit, initial inventory, training, digital setup, and 90-day onboarding support. No hidden costs. The projected payback period is 18–30 months.
Which cities are available for a Lahori Adda franchise? Lahori Adda
Phase 1 is now open across Panipat, Karnal, Sonipat, Kurukshetra, and Ambala — the NH-44 corridor in Haryana. Slots are limited per city. Phase 2 (Rohtak, Hisar, Yamunanagar, Faridabad) opens after Phase 1 is complete. Apply now to secure your territory.
Do I need prior hospitality experience to open a Lahori Adda? Lahori Adda
No. We have trained complete first-timers to run successful Lahori Adda outlets. Our 90-day onboarding covers every aspect — kitchen operations, service standards, digital marketing, Zomato/Swiggy setup, and staff management. What matters is the right location, adequate capital, and genuine motivation.
What does a restaurant website from Anandam include? Website
Every website includes: custom design (not a template), full online menu with photos and pricing, table booking or reservation integration, WhatsApp ordering button, Google Maps location, SEO setup to rank for "restaurant in [your city]", mobile-optimised design, and social media links. The site is fully handed over to you — you own it, no monthly fees to us.
How long does a website take to build and go live? Website
From brief to live website: typically 14 days. This includes design, content setup, SEO optimisation, and launch. We need: your brand details (name, logo if available), menu, photos or the brief to source stock photos, and the domain name (we can help purchase if needed).
How much does a restaurant website cost? Website
Website pricing is based on the scope — a standard restaurant website with menu and booking typically starts at ₹25,000. Larger projects with e-commerce, multiple pages, or custom features are quoted separately. Contact us with your requirements for a transparent, fixed-price quote. No monthly fees charged by us after handover.
Will my website rank on Google? Website
Every website we build includes foundational SEO — correct page titles, meta descriptions, local business structured data, Google My Business integration, and page speed optimisation. Combined with a Google My Business profile (which we set up as part of the package), most of our clients appear in Google's local search within 2–4 weeks of launch.
