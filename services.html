FAQs

Service questions answered

The most common things people ask us before they start.

Do you work with first-time restaurant owners?
Yes — in fact many of our best outcomes have been with first-time owners. You don't bring bad habits. We bring 35 years of expertise. Together the results are excellent.
How long before I see results?
Most clients see measurable revenue improvement within weeks 5–8. The full transformation is visible by end of engagement. We commit to a minimum 25% improvement by Engagement end.
Do you only work in Haryana?
No — our primary market is North India (Haryana, Punjab, Delhi NCR, UP) but we take projects anywhere in India. Digital services like marketing and website design can be done fully remotely for any city.
How much does a consulting programme cost?
Pricing is customised based on your outlet size, location, and the services required. The consultation is always free. We give you a transparent quote before any commitment is made.
What if we don't see the promised results?
Our engagements consistently deliver meaningful revenue improvement. If we don't deliver, we continue working without additional charge until we do. This isn't a marketing line — it's in the agreement.
Can I hire you for just one specific thing?
Yes. All six services are available as standalone projects — website, staff training, marketing setup, outlet launch, or expansion planning. You don't need to take the full consulting programme.

Yes — Lahori Adda is our own chole bhature franchise brand, currently franchising across Haryana's NH-44 corridor. Limited Franchise Slots. See the Lahori Adda page →
How is Anandam different from other consultants?
35 years of real hospitality experience — not MBA graduates with spreadsheets. Our founder has personally opened restaurants, managed teams of hundreds, and run large food operations. We know what actually works on the ground.
